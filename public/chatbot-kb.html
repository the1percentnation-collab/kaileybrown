Chatbot Knowledge Base

Add facts, FAQs, policies, or any information you want the chatbot to know. Entries are injected into every conversation automatically.

How it works: Each entry has a Title (a short label, like "Refund Policy" or "About Coach Marcus") and a Body (the actual information). The chatbot reads all active entries every time a user sends a message. Use the Order field to control which entries appear first.

Books belong somewhere else: titles, blurbs, buy links, ISBNs and reading guides have their own structured editor — Books & Buy Links. Put them there rather than in a free-text entry, so the chatbot is told those links are the only ones it may give out.
