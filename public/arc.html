How it works

  1. 01

    Apply

    Pick the book you want to read and send a quick application. It takes less than a minute.

  2. 02

    Watch your inbox

    I review every application myself. If you’re approved, you’ll get an email with your download link and simple instructions for sending it to your Kindle.

  3. 03

    Read & review

    Read it, then leave an honest review on Amazon, Goodreads, or both.

The ARC Promise

Thanks for reading early! By joining, you agree to:

  • Keep it private. Don’t share your login, or copy, screenshot, or repost the book.
  • Be honest. Reviews don’t have to be positive, just genuine.
  • Skip spoilers. Tag or hide any big twists.
  • Disclose your copy. Add “I received an advance copy and am voluntarily leaving an honest review.”
  • Leave a review on Amazon and/or Goodreads when you finish. No penalty if you fall behind, just a friendly reminder.

Leave a review

Reviews are how readers find new authors. Every review you leave, even just a sentence or two, helps another reader discover a book they’ll love and helps me keep writing them. Thank you for being here.
