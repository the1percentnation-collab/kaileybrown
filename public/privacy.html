4. Text Messaging (SMS) & Consent

If you provide a mobile number and consent to receive text messages, we (or an account admin managing their contacts) may send SMS messages. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message; reply HELP for help. We honor STOP requests and will not send further marketing texts to a number that has opted out. We maintain records of consent as required by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA).
